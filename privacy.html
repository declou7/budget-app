Privacy and data security

Last updated October 12, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers a private budgeting app used only by Luis Decamps and his spouse to manage their own household finances. The app has no other users and is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app reads

  • Balances and transactions of the bank accounts and credit cards we choose to connect, received through Plaid.
  • Details we type in ourselves, such as bills, budgets, savings goals, and the debts and interest rates we want to track.

The app never receives our bank usernames or passwords. We sign in on our bank's own screen inside Plaid's connection window.

How the data is used

Only to show the two of us our own spending, bills, balances and debt payoff plan. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share this data. The only outside services involved are:

  • Plaid, which securely connects the app to our banks under its own privacy policy.
  • Tailscale, which provides the encrypted private network we use to reach the app from our phones.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces the app's pages display.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

  • The data is stored in a database on a computer in our home. It is not kept on a public server.
  • The credentials that let the app read our bank data (access tokens) are encrypted before they are stored.
  • The app can only be opened by the two of us. It requires a personal sign-in, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • The app is reached only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection on a private network. It is not open to the public internet.
  • Each of us can see the household view, but can change only our own accounts and entries.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Data stays in the app while we use it. If we disconnect a bank, the app removes that bank's accounts and transactions. We can also ask Plaid to remove a connection at any time, and we can delete the app's database at any time.
